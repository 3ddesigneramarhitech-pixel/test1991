Manufacturer Website Intake Checklist

Share the details below and we can craft a tailored manufacturer website. This list covers branding, product lines, compliance, and marketing essentials so your site launches quickly and confidently.

Information Needed

Category Details to Provide Examples / Notes
Company Overview Legal business name, headquarters location, founding year, mission statement. Include a short company story or milestones.
Branding Logo files, brand colors, typography preferences, tone of voice. Provide vector logos (SVG/AI) and any brand guidelines.
Product & Services Full product list, key specs, differentiators, pricing guidance. Highlight flagship products or core service lines.
Industries Served Primary industries, target customer profiles, geographic markets. List certifications or approvals required in those markets.
Manufacturing Capabilities Facility locations, capacity, materials handled, equipment list. Include photos of facilities or production lines.
Quality & Compliance ISO certifications, QA process, safety standards, compliance documents. Upload PDFs or provide links to certificates.
Case Studies Project summaries, results, customer quotes, before/after metrics. Share logos of approved clients where possible.
Media Assets Product photos, factory imagery, team headshots, videos. High-resolution images (minimum 2000px wide).
Website Goals Primary conversions (RFQs, calls, downloads), KPIs, success metrics. Define 1-3 primary actions for visitors.
Contact Details Sales email, phone, locations, support hours, social links. List regional contacts if you have multiple sales teams.
Marketing & SEO Target keywords, competitors, existing marketing collateral. Provide brochures, product sheets, or ads.
Legal Requirements Privacy policy, terms & conditions, regulatory statements. Share any required legal copy or disclaimers.

Once you share the information above, I can draft wireframes, page copy, and a launch-ready site plan.
